# Code of Conduct

CommitCamp is built for people making their first contributions, so we hold ourselves to a high standard of patience and respect.

## Our standards

Do:

- Assume good intent, and assume the other person is new.
- Explain the *why* in review comments, and point to docs rather than just saying "wrong".
- Give credit, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion about the work.

Don't:

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately, for any reason, including their experience level.
- Use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances.
- Publish anyone's private information without their permission.
- Mock a question. There are no stupid questions here.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews on this repository, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer at the email on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/aaronanish-t), or through GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and contributions that violate this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, depending on severity.
